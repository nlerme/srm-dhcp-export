# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, focus on technical merit, accept constructive review, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commits, and other spaces managed by the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, Nicolas Lermé, at <nicolas.lerme@gmail.com>. The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the project community.
